# KreditBee

> KreditBee is a Bengaluru, India digital lending platform operated by Finnovation Tech Solutions
> Private Limited (CIN U74900KA2016PTC086953), lending through its in-house RBI-registered NBFC
> KrazyBee Services Limited and through partner banks and NBFCs. It offers instant personal loans
> (Rs 6,000 to Rs 10 lakh), business loans (up to Rs 1 crore), two-wheeler loans, loan against
> property, a flexi personal loan line, 24K digital gold and free credit score reports, delivered
> through Android and iOS apps and a single-page web application.
